# Audio credits

- `pour-water.mp3`: “Cold water in mug #1” (sound #1156), recorded by Joseph SARDIN for BigSoundBank. Released under CC0 (public domain); attribution is not required. Original MP3 retained unedited. The game plays short overlapping slices of it: the glass moves through the recording as it fills, the bottle loops the heaviest pour, and sloshes and spills reuse brief excerpts.
- Source and license: https://bigsoundbank.com/cold-water-in-mug-s1156.html
- Download: https://bigsoundbank.com/UPLOAD/mp3/1156.mp3

Every other sound (cap pop, drips, clink) is synthesized in `index.html` with the Web Audio API.
